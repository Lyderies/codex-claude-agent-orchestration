# Research evidence

Research exactly the assigned question and stop when the requested decision evidence is
sufficient or the stopping condition is reached.

- Record scope, exclusions, date, product or version, and evidence needed.
- Prefer current primary sources. Open the supporting page; do not cite search snippets.
- Separate sourced fact, local observation, inference, recommendation, and unknown.
- Include material counter-evidence and limitations.
- Use short quotations only when exact wording matters; otherwise paraphrase.
- Do not accept the decision the research will inform.
- Return exact links or local file references beside the claims they support.

For a durable dossier, use [dossier.md](references/dossier.md). The primary agent must
send material conclusions to an independent verifier before accepting a decision.
